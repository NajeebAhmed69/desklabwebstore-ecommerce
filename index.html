Our story

Built for the space you work in every day.

DeskLab PK started with a simple observation: students, software developers, and remote professionals in Pakistan spend 8 to 10 hours a day at their desks, yet affordable, matching workspace accessories are scattered and overpriced.

Why we started DeskLab

A cluttered desk with tangled power bricks, poor lighting, and low laptop screens leads to neck strain and constant distraction. We curated a cohesive matte-black and Nordic grey ecosystem so you can upgrade your entire setup from one trusted local store—starting at Rs. 1,199.

Planned Business Idea

DeskLab solves the problem of messy, uncomfortable desks by offering affordable, matching matte-black and Nordic grey accessories in one store—starting at Rs. 1,199 with Cash on Delivery across Pakistan.

Marketing & Sale Strategy

  • 15% Student Discount: Verified at checkout with a .edu.pk email or roll number to grow through campus word-of-mouth.
  • Flash Sale Hooks: Discounts on the LED Light Bars (Rs. 3,999) and 75% Mechanical Keyboard (Rs. 6,999) to attract clicks.
  • Short-Form Videos: Simple TikTok and Instagram Reels showing clean desk setups and cable organization.

Sourcing, Inventory & Shipping

  • Local Wholesale Sourcing: Stock purchased in small batches from wholesale markets in Karachi (Saddar/Techno City) and Lahore (Hall Road) with realistic 25%–30% retail markups.
  • Home Micro-Inventory: Stored at home in Hyderabad/Karachi with zero shop or warehouse rent, keeping ~80–100 units on hand.
  • Flat Rs. 300 Shipping: Shipped via local COD couriers (PostEx / Trax / Leopards), covering the Rs. 250–300 courier cost.

Simple Monthly Revenue Model

At a realistic starting pace of 60 orders/month (~2 orders/day) (2.5% conversion on 2,400 visitors) and an average order of Rs. 3,800, DeskLab makes Rs. 228,000/month in gross sales. After product costs (~Rs. 150,000), courier fees (~Rs. 17,000), packaging (~Rs. 3,000), and social media ads (~Rs. 16,000), net profit is ~Rs. 42,000/month.
